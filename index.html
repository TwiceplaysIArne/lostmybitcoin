The game

What is Lost My Bitcoin?

A first-person search and tycoon game with a low-poly look and a big sense of humour.

Somewhere in a giant landfill, under 12,000 trash bags, lies a hard drive with 100 Bitcoin on it: THE DRIVE. You start with nothing but a pair of gloves. Rip open bags, dig for finds, sell the junk and put the money back into better tools.

Bag by bag, you go from one pair of gloves to running a fully automated recycling plant. The goal never changes: find THE DRIVE. And when you finally hold it in your hands, one question is left: SELL or HODL?

Features

From gloves to a recycling plant

Everything you need to dig through a mountain of trash, and then make machines do it for you.

  • Rip open trash bags

    Tear bags open by hand and dig through the trash for finds. Only 12,000 to go.

  • Sell junk, buy upgrades

    Sell your junk at the dealers. Spend the money on tools, upgrades and machines in the shop.

  • Crack hard drives

    Scan drives, recover data and crack passwords. Most hold a few small coins, none of them Bitcoin. Sell the coins at the coin exchange.

  • Build a recycling plant

    Conveyor belts, sorters, bag rippers, presses, a bag cannon and a crane. Automate everything.

  • Heavy machinery

    Move whole mountains of trash with loaders and an excavator.

  • Hire staff

    Hire workers who sort trash for you. Run out of money for their wages and they go on strike.

  • Fresh trash keeps coming

    Garbage trucks and a cargo plane bring new trash. Random events like rain shake things up.

  • Co-op for up to 4 players

    Search the landfill together with up to three friends.

And more: a contracts board, a collection book, achievements, a house with a server farm, a character creator and emotes. Play in English or German.

Scam warning

The game has Bitcoin in its name, and scammers love that. So, to be very clear:

  • There is no Lost My Bitcoin coin, token, NFT or airdrop.
  • The team never sends you a direct message first.
  • The team never asks for your wallet, seed phrase, passwords or money.

If anyone offers you a Lost My Bitcoin coin, token, NFT or airdrop, messages you first, or asks for any of these things, it is a scam. Do not click their links. Report them to the moderators on Discord or r/LostBitcoinGame.
